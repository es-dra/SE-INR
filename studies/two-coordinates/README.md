Notation: `s` = scale, `r` = HR frequency (cycles/HR pixel, Chebyshev radius), `u = 2·s·r` = frequency over the LR Nyquist, `R(u) = 1 − err/target energy` per band, `m` = cell multiplier (cell = m × native), `h = 2(m−1)/s` = per-axis step in the actual decoder cell coordinate. For output change δ and native error e, response energy `J = ||δ||²` and error change `D = J − 2Re⟨e,δ⟩` are different quantities.

## Prospective equal-step response (stage 17)

The [protocol](17-equal-step-response/protocol.json), [predictions](17-equal-step-response/predictions.json), input identities and scorer were frozen before inference. [FROZEN.sha256](17-equal-step-response/FROZEN.sha256) has SHA256 `a77f84a54fb09676a6e6703b345ff8ce30d42597bfc874f29561bff552f9fb06`. The integrated run→score pipeline completed in 57.5 minutes with all 17,712 records (246 images × 6 checkpoints × 4 scales × 3 steps). Common top-left modcrop-12 HR support and a four-pixel shave are fixed across scales; raw float32 outputs and complete frequency accounting include DC and the exact Nyquist boundary.

[Formal scores](17-equal-step-response/score/summary.json) retain all 36 adjacent comparisons and three frozen verdicts. Bootstrap resamples 10,000 paired image sets within each dataset, after averaging the six fixed checkpoints. The global gate uses a one-sided 0.05/36 lower bound above 1.10. The 12-condition gate instead uses 0.05/3 within each condition. The Set14 endpoint gate uses one-sided 0.025 bounds for its two positive steps; the failed h = +0.25 result is inconclusive, not evidence of the opposite direction.

The clearest response-versus-cost example is BSD100 at h = +0.5: J₆/J₂ = 1.237 [1.059, 1.439], while D decreases from 79.42 to 40.77 ×10⁻⁶ MSE, with paired ΔD = −38.65 [−55.13, −22.01] ×10⁻⁶. These are descriptive 95% intervals, separate from the corrected prediction gates. Finite interventions compare different LR features and native conditions. Some negative-step cells and native ×6 fall outside the training cell interval; the result does not isolate a scale-only Jacobian.

[Frequency summaries](17-equal-step-response/score/frequency_summary.json) describe all 48 dataset–step–scale conditions after scoring. At h = +0.5, the high-frequency share of J (r ≥ 0.375) decreases from 43.5–50.7% at ×2 to 2.4–3.0% at ×6; the middle band [0.1875, 0.375) accounts for 60.5–66.7% at ×6. DC is separate, the low band is (0, 0.1875), and the high band includes r = 0.5. This frequency description has no additional pass/fail gate.

[Independent verification](17-equal-step-response/score/independent_validation.json) resampled the per-image measurements directly without importing the frozen scorer. Corrected bounds agree to 1.11×10⁻¹⁵, ordinary 95% intervals to 1.78×10⁻¹⁵, and complete-frequency sums to 1.28×10⁻¹⁵. Its [script](17-equal-step-response/verify_completed.py) and the compact results are retained locally; expanded records and CSV tables remain at their SHA-identified server paths.

## Prospective training controls (stage 18)

The [protocol](18-cell-training-source/protocol.json), [predictions](18-cell-training-source/predictions.json), [scorer](18-cell-training-source/score.py), data identities and source hashes were frozen before training. [FROZEN.sha256](18-cell-training-source/FROZEN.sha256) has SHA256 `b601cf3d197cc81bfe0e6691f1c310457c8c936e49985e7e073062e4e06c1fb1`. Each of the four train/evaluation slots has a hard 24-hour limit and fixed epoch-200 checkpoint; failures are retained rather than replaced.

The constant arm receives the mean actual decoder cell, c₀ = E[96/round(48s)] = 0.9242188453 for s uniform on [1,4]. The shuffled arm assigns complete cell vectors by batch derangement, preserving the marginal and both input dimensions. A separate random generator preserves the shared data/model RNG streams. [Synthetic checks](18-cell-training-source/synthetic_validation.json) verify geometry, initial weights, data streams and marginal preservation. Queries/features retain other scale cues, so these arms test correctly paired explicit cell information rather than the absence of all scale information.

Primary evaluation uses full-image Y-PSNR on Set5, Set14, BSD100 and Urban100 at ×2/3/4/6, with the original PIL-bicubic degradation, clipping and scale-dependent shave. R(u) uses the original DIV2K crop cohort, antialiased Torch resizing, unclipped outputs and a four-pixel shave. Native and no-cell checkpoints are reevaluated identically. Shuffled-cell primary evaluation receives native cell; fixed-mean and four independent marginal assignments are predeclared sensitivity checks. Bootstrap intervals resample paired images within datasets and condition on two trained seeds. Results are pending.

The separate [independent checker](18-cell-training-source/verify_completed.py) runs only after the complete formal pipeline. It verifies the 50,328-row grid, checkpoint identities, intervention audits and frozen gates without importing the scorer. Its synthetic preflight passed. Supplemental descriptions add paired intervals for the predeclared equal-bin recovery regions and report all six shuffled inference policies; they introduce no new prediction gates. Recovery is computed by pooling image energies within each seed, then averaging the two seed-specific ratios. Regional summaries average quarter-u-bin differences and are not region-pooled energy ratios. A T1 pass requires a point estimate of at least 0.05 dB and a corrected interval excluding zero; it does not require the interval's lower bound to exceed 0.05 dB.

## Retrospective scalar and residual analyses (stages 15–16)

Transfer-function analysis (`01-cell-mismatch/transfer.py`) motivated the approximation `P_m ≈ w_m(r)·P_1`. The **2026-10-23 offline audit qualifies its scale-independence and explanatory claims**: the fitted strength w changes with scale even when the affected frequency range is similar. For doubled cell, the original proxy Q = Σ(1−w)²RE is 43.6%, 71.9%, 81.5%, and 250.1% of the measured DIV2K cost at ×2/3/4/6. Agreement at ×3/4 includes cancellation between omitted terms and does not establish an exact or prospective law. Small mismatch (m = 4/3) gives a positive proxy but a slightly negative measured cost at ×4/6; halved cell has only 9.7–19.7% proxy coverage.

The exact retained-band accounting is D = Q + G + H, where G = Σ2w(1−w)Re⟨T−P,P⟩ and H = Σ(||ε||²−2Re⟨T−P,ε⟩), ε = P_m−wP. A symmetric two-factor decomposition of ΔQ separates R and w while retaining ΔG and ΔH. For ×2→×6, the actual-cost shares [R,w,G,H] are [22.69,18.68,4.22,54.41]% on DIV2K and [27.26,14.27,−0.04,58.51]% on Set14. The Set14 H share has a paired image-bootstrap 95% interval of [41.99,66.96]%; intervals condition on the two existing trained seeds. Fitting w per image still leaves H at 53.71% of the endpoint decrease, so pooling images alone does not close the gap.

These are descriptive **RGB MSE** decompositions on 240-pixel crops, with the original 16 r bands (0<r<0.5, excluding DC and the exact Nyquist outer ring). They are not dB component shares or full-image benchmark claims. Set14 includes Parseval checks, the omitted-frequency audit, all-non-DC sensitivity, and 2,000 paired image bootstrap samples; DIV2K v11 has aggregate statistics only, so no image CI can be recovered for it. All old results remain unchanged. The analysis does not identify the training benefit of cell.
