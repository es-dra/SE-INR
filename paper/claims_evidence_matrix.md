| Analytic sinc response contributes to the final candidate. | `SC-INR-NoSinc` seed1 benchmark in `artifacts/derived/analysis/benchmark_progress_2026-05-10/seed1_signed_phiz_nosinc_summary.csv`: vs full `SC-INR`, ID `-0.1397 dB`, OOD `-0.0467 dB`, All `-0.0777 dB`; NoSinc OOD remains `+0.0382 dB` vs LTE.
